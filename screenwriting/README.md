# Screenwriting

Turn Claude into a screenwriting partner that works the way studios, production companies and streamers expect. Built for screenwriters, actors, directors and producers, in English, Spanish or any language.

## What it does
- Develops a project in stages: logline, synopsis, beat sheet, outline, script and rewrite, with your approval between each step.
- Writes in industry-standard screenplay format (scene headings, action, dialogue, extensions) and delivers Fountain files that open in Final Draft, Highland, Fade In and WriterSolo, or a formatted PDF.
- Builds a pitch package: treatment, series bible, pitch deck and query letter.
- Gives studio-style coverage of a script with a Recommend / Consider / Pass verdict.

## Getting started
Install the plugin, then ask Claude something like "Help me develop a feature about a female boxer" or "Give my pilot studio-style coverage."

## Skills
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `studio-screenplay` | Develop, write, format and review film and TV screenplays and pitch materials |

## Data and privacy
This plugin contains text instructions only. It runs no code, connects to no external services, and sends or stores no data outside your conversation with Claude.
