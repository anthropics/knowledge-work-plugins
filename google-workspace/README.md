# Google Workspace

Connect Claude to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and other Google Workspace apps in one place. With these connectors, Claude can help with your email, find and read files, and check your schedule, within the access you grant.

## Included connectors

| Connector | What it gives Claude access to |
|-----------|--------------------------------|
| Gmail | Your email |
| Google Drive | Files stored in your Drive |
| Google Calendar | Your calendars and events |
| Other Google Workspace apps | Varies by app; which ones you can connect depends on your account |

These connectors are hosted by Google.

## Setup

Installing this plugin does not connect anything by itself. In Claude, the plugin uses the Google connectors from Claude's connector settings:

- If you have already connected one of them, the plugin uses that connection rather than adding a second copy.
- Connect the others you want, where available, with your Google account in Claude's connector settings.
- If your organization controls which connectors members can use, an admin may need to enable a connector first.

## Permissions

What each connector may do is set on the connector itself: you can allow, ask, or block each tool. Access to your Google data is also limited to what you approve on Google's sign-in screen, and you can disconnect a connector at any time from Claude's connector settings.
